# Phase 13: Order Fulfillment & Operations (SOP)
**Brand:** Snagged (Indian DTC Creator Tech)
**Supplier App:** GlowRoad / Roposo Clout

## Step 1: Order Reception
When a customer submits the Cash On Delivery (COD) form on `getsnagged.in`, it generates a pre-filled WhatsApp message sent directly to your phone.

**Example Message:**
> Hi Snagged Team,
> COD Order #SNG-482910
> Item: Snagged 360 AI Mount
> Amount: ₹1,999
> Deliver to: Arjun Mehta, Flat 402, Kothrud, Pune - 411038
> Phone: 9876543210

## Step 2: Verification (The Anti-RTO Step)
**DO NOT process the order immediately.** You must verify intent to reduce RTO (Return to Origin) losses.
Reply to the customer using this template:
> "Hi Arjun! 👋 We received your COD order for the Snagged AI Mount (₹1,999). Reply 'CONFIRM' and we will dispatch it today via express shipping. No advance payment required."

*If they do not reply within 12 hours, send a follow-up. If no reply after 24 hours, cancel the order.*

## Step 3: Dropship Fulfillment
Once the customer replies "CONFIRM":
1. Open the GlowRoad / Roposo Clout app.
2. Search for the exact product (e.g., "360 Auto Tracking Gimbal").
3. Click **Buy Now / Add to Cart**.
4. Enter the **Customer's Address** (copy-pasted from WhatsApp).
5. Select **Cash on Delivery (COD)**.
6. Enter your **Selling Price** (e.g., ₹1,999).
   * *The app will automatically calculate your profit margin (Selling Price - Sourcing Price) and deposit it to your bank account after successful delivery.*
7. Place the order.

## Step 4: Tracking & Customer Updates
1. GlowRoad will generate an AWB (Tracking Number) within 24 hours.
2. Message the customer:
   > "Your Snagged AI Mount is packed and dispatched! 🚀 Track your package here: [Tracking Link]. Have ₹1,999 ready in cash or UPI for the delivery executive."
3. If the courier attempts delivery and the customer is unavailable, message the customer immediately to reschedule.

## Financial Flow
* You spend: ₹0 upfront.
* Courier collects: ₹1,999.
* Supplier keeps: ~₹500 (product cost + shipping).
* **Your Bank Account receives:** ~₹1,499 profit (deposited ~3-5 days post-delivery).
