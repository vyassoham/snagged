7-Day Hassle-Free Replacement Policy · Effective September 2026

1. 7-Day Replacement Guarantee

We test every hardware piece before packaging. If your item arrives physically damaged, functionally defective, or missing accessories, you are entitled to a 100% Free Replacement within 7 days of delivery.

2. How to Request a Replacement

Raising a replacement request takes less than 2 minutes:

  1. Take a short 30-second video showing the issue or defect.
  2. Send it to our WhatsApp Support team at +91 9210023153 along with your Order ID.
  3. Our team will verify the request within 4 to 6 business hours and arrange a free reverse pickup or immediately dispatch a brand-new replacement unit.

3. Refund Guidelines for COD Orders

If a replacement unit is unavailable or out of stock, we initiate a 100% full refund. For Cash on Delivery orders, funds are deposited directly to your verified UPI ID or Bank Account via IMPS within 3 business days.

4. Exclusions

Products damaged due to drops, water submersion, intentional physical tampering, or requests made after 7 days from the delivery date are not eligible for replacement.
