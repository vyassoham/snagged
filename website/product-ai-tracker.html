Honest Disclosure

This device is not for everyone.

  • Need cinema-grade stabilisation? Snagged tracks and pans — it doesn't stabilise handheld shake. It's a smart rotating mount, not a gimbal stabiliser.
  • Film from a fixed position? A basic tripod serves you better for half the price. Snagged earns its cost when you move.
  • Want an app dashboard? There is no app. No telemetry. No data leaving your phone. That's the point.
