Privacy Policy

Information Technology Act 2000 Compliance · September 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order on Snagged, we collect only the necessary information required to process and ship your parcel: your full name, shipping delivery address, contact mobile phone number, and city/pincode. We do not sell or monetize your personal information to third parties.

2. How Your Data Is Used

  • Delivery Fulfillment: Shared strictly with licensed courier executives (e.g. Delhivery, BlueDart) to deliver your physical order.
  • Order Verification: To communicate shipment dispatches and delivery updates via WhatsApp and SMS.
  • Customer Support: To assist you with replacements or inquiries.
