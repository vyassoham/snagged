About Snagged

We started because we were tired of asking strangers to hold our phones.

Snagged was born out of a specific frustration: two creators in India who spent more time setting up shots than actually filming. Every "smart" gadget we tried needed an app, a Bluetooth connection, or a firmware update before it worked. Most of them didn't track reliably past 2 metres.

So we started sourcing and testing hardware that prioritises function over features. Products that work the moment you turn them on. No accounts. No logins. No data leaving your device.

Every product in the Snagged catalog is something we personally use, have tested for durability, and would hand to a friend without a disclaimer. If a product doesn't meet that bar, it doesn't make it to the site.

We're two partners, building from India, for Indian creators.

Support

Questions about any product?
We'd rather answer here.

What payment methods do you accept? +

All products ship via Cash on Delivery (COD). At the doorstep, you can pay via cash or UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm). We're working on integrating prepaid online payments — once our GST registration is complete, we'll add Razorpay for cards and UPI checkout.

What is your return and replacement policy? +

Every product has a 7-Day Free Replacement policy for transit damage or hardware defects. Send us an unboxing video via WhatsApp and we'll dispatch a replacement. No troubleshooting hoops.

How long does delivery take? +

3–5 business days for metro cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata). 5–7 days for Tier 2/3 cities. Up to 9 days for remote or North-East regions. All orders dispatch within 24 hours of WhatsApp confirmation.

How do I contact support? +

WhatsApp is our primary channel — fastest response, usually within 30 minutes during 10 AM – 7 PM IST. You can also email support@getsnagged.in for non-urgent queries.
