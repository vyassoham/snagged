# Legal & Compliance Policies for Snagged (India E-Commerce)

Under the **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020** in India, e-commerce entities are required to disclose clear details regarding returns, refunds, exchange, warranty, delivery, modes of payment, and grievance redressal mechanism.

---

## 1. SHIPPING & DELIVERY POLICY (`shipping_policy.md`)

```markdown
# Shipping & Delivery Policy — Snagged

Last updated: September 2026

Thank you for visiting and shopping at **Snagged** (getsnagged.in). Below are the terms and conditions that constitute our Shipping Policy.

### 1. Shipment Processing Time
* All orders are processed within **24 to 48 business hours** of order confirmation. 
* For **Cash on Delivery (COD)** orders, processing begins only after phone/WhatsApp confirmation from our verification team.
* Orders are not processed or shipped on Sundays or national holidays.

### 2. Shipping Rates & Delivery Estimates
* **Standard Shipping:** FREE across all serviceable pin codes in India.
* **Express Delivery:** Estimated **3 to 7 business days** depending on the destination:
  - **Tier 1 Metro Cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune):** 3–5 business days.
  - **Tier 2 & Tier 3 Cities:** 4–7 business days.
  - **Rest of India (Remote/North-East):** 6–9 business days.

### 3. Shipment Confirmation & Tracking
Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive an SMS, WhatsApp message, and email containing your **Airway Bill (AWB) tracking number** and courier tracking link (BlueDart / Delhivery / Xpressbees / Shadowfax). Tracking information updates within 24 hours of dispatch.

### 4. Cash on Delivery (COD) Rules
* COD is available across 90%+ pin codes in India.
* Please ensure you or an authorized person is available at the provided address with the exact cash amount or UPI ready for the delivery executive.
* Multiple unaccepted COD deliveries from the same customer or address will result in blacklisting from future COD purchases.

### 5. Damages & Lost Shipments
If your package arrives visibly tampered or damaged from the outside:
1. Do NOT accept the parcel, or
2. Take a video while unboxing the parcel and immediately send it to our support team at **support@getsnagged.in** or WhatsApp at **+91-XXXXXXXXXX** within 48 hours of delivery.
```

## 2. RETURN, REPLACEMENT & REFUND POLICY (`refund_policy.md`)

```markdown
# Return, Replacement & Refund Policy — Snagged

At Snagged, we pride ourselves on providing high-quality creator and tech accessories. Every unit undergoes quality inspection before dispatch.

### 1. 7-Day Replacement Policy
We offer a **7-Day Free Replacement** for products that arrive:
* Physically damaged during transit.
* Functionally defective or with technical hardware malfunction.
* Missing parts or accessories.
* Incorrect item delivered.

### 2. Eligibility for Replacement
To be eligible for a replacement:
* The request must be raised within **7 days of delivery**.
* An **unboxing video** is mandatory showing the shipping label, product condition, and the issue experienced.
* The product must be returned with its original retail packaging, cables, documentation, and all accessories.

### 3. Non-Returnable Scenarios
* Damage resulting from misuse, drops, water damage, or unauthorized repairs.
* Wear and tear from standard usage.
* Requests made after 7 days from the delivery date.

### 4. Refund Process
* **Prepaid Orders:** Refunds are credited back to the original source (Credit Card / UPI / Netbanking) within **5–7 business days** of receiving and inspecting the returned unit at our hub.
* **Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders:** For verified returns of COD orders, refunds are disbursed directly to your verified **Bank Account via NEFT/IMPS or UPI** within 3–5 business days after inspection.
```
