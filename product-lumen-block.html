Why This Exists

Good lighting shouldn't require a C-stand.

Ring lights are bulky, hard to transport, and impossible to hide in a scene. The L1 Lumen Block was built for creators who shoot on location. It fits in your pocket, but more importantly, it solves the mounting problem.

The entire back panel is magnetic. Snap it onto a metal doorframe, a fridge, or your car hood. It also includes a standard cold-shoe mount to slide onto your camera or Snagged AI Tracker.
