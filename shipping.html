Shipping & Delivery

Effective Date: September 2026 · Compliant with Indian E-Commerce Rules

1. Order Processing Time

All confirmed orders are processed and handed over to our courier partners within 24 to 48 business hours. For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, dispatch processing commences immediately after WhatsApp/phone confirmation from our verification team.

2. Pan-India Delivery Estimates & Shipping Charges

We provide 100% FREE Standard Shipping across all serviceable pincodes in India. We partner with Tier-1 logistics providers (Delhivery, BlueDart, Shadowfax, Xpressbees).

Metro Cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai):3 to 5 business days
Tier 2 & Tier 3 Cities:4 to 7 business days
Remote Locations & North-East:6 to 9 business days

3. Tracking Your Order

Once dispatched, you will receive an automated tracking link and Airway Bill (AWB) number on WhatsApp. You can monitor the real-time movement of your parcel at every checkpoint.

4. Cash on Delivery (COD) Protocol

COD is available across 19,000+ Indian pincodes. Please ensure you or an authorized family member is available at the provided address with exact cash or UPI ready when the delivery executive arrives.

5. Damaged or Tampered Shipments

If the outer packaging appears heavily torn or tampered, do not accept the package from the courier. If accepted, please record a continuous unboxing video and notify our WhatsApp support team within 48 hours at +91 9210023153.
